I’m Eduardo — a Brazilian designer based in Berlin, working at the intersection of branding and product design.

Currently at Axel Springer Tech, I worked with brands like Bild, WELT, Business Insider, and the Telegraph. I enjoy working on design systems, experience design, product workflows and whatever teams need to create better products and services. Previously, I worked in social studies and graphic design, experiences that shape how I see the world and my profession.

Outside of work I climb, play music, and collect records. Lately, I’m interested in AI and its capabilities to empower designers and teams, while also being aware of the risks and challenges it brings to our society.
